The problem

Elderly Fall Protection: Comfort vs. Safety

Elderly fall-protection wearables often force a trade-off between comfort and protection. Traditional padded hip protectors can reduce injury risk during falls, but they're often bulky, heavy, hot, and uncomfortable for continuous daily use. Because of this, many elderly users avoid wearing them — leaving them vulnerable to serious fall-related injuries.

01Falls are a major safety concern for elderly people.
02Protective equipment is effective only when people actually wear it.
03Bulky, uncomfortable designs lead to low adoption and poor compliance.
04A comfortable, lightweight solution could provide continuous protection without interfering with daily life.
The solution

Smart Adaptive Fall-Protection Wearable

A lightweight, breathable smart wearable that combines fall detection with adaptive impact protection. Motion sensors and AI identify sudden movements associated with a fall and activate protective cushioning around vulnerable areas such as the hips — with an alert sent to a caregiver the moment it matters.

"Protection when needed, comfort when not."
ComfortableDesigned for everyday use without bulky padding.
SmartUses sensors and AI-based fall detection.
AdaptiveProtection activates only when needed, not all the time.
ConnectedNotifies caregivers after a detected fall.
PreventiveEncourages continuous wear, reducing fall consequences.
How the solution works
1

Continuous Motion Monitoring

IMU sensors (accelerometer + gyroscope) track movement, balance, speed, and body orientation at all times.

2

AI Fall Detection

An AI/ML algorithm distinguishes normal activity — walking, sitting, bending — from patterns associated with a fall.

3

Fall Prediction / Detection

On an unusual movement pattern, the system rapidly estimates whether a fall is likely.

4

Instant Protection Activation

Compact protective cushioning deploys around vulnerable areas such as the hips.

5

Impact Protection

The activated mechanism absorbs and distributes impact, reducing the likelihood of serious injury.

6

Emergency Alert

A caregiver or family member is notified through a connected smartphone or communication module.

7

Reset & Reuse

The protective system resets, returning the wearable to normal, comfortable operation.

Movement→ Sensors→ AI Analysis→ Fall Detected→ Protection Activated→ Impact Reduced→ Caregiver Alert
